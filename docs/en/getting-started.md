# Getting started with Bloodborne Legendary Run

[Français](../premiers-pas.md) · **English**

## Choose how to access the app

- **Web.** Open the [official site](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en) in your browser. You can browse the [Hunter’s Guide](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en/gallery) without an account.
- **Windows.** Select the Windows installer on the [download page](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en/download) and open the downloaded file. Then launch Bloodborne Legendary Run from the Start menu and sign in.
- **Android.** The [closed beta page](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en/beta) explains how to request an invitation. Use the Google Play account that received the invite.

## Track a character

1. [Create an account](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en/register) or [sign in](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en/login) to save your progress.
2. On the [constellation](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en/sky), choose a canvas and open a star to read its objective.
3. Complete objectives as you play. Follow the instructions for each challenge and attach a screenshot where the app offers that option.
4. If you play multiple characters, use the character picker in **My account** to create or resume a run.

## Use the guide while you play

Open the entry linked to a challenge or browse the [Hunter’s Guide](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en/gallery). You can also go straight to [Blood Gems](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en/gallery/gemmes) or [Chalice Dungeons](https://bloodborne-legendary-run.com/en/gallery/calices). Some entries contain spoilers for bosses, quests, or late-game areas.

Found a broken link or an unclear step? Visit the [support guide](../../.github/en/SUPPORT.md).
